How VeriClaim Works

Simple explanation of our verification process

1. Live News Search

When you enter a claim, we simultaneously search 7 global news wire services (The Guardian, NewsAPI, Currents, Mediastack, GNews, NewsData, and WorldNews) to find recent stories about that topic.

2. Subject & Object Entity Verification

We require that all core subjects and objects in the claim (e.g. both "Elon Musk" and "Google") co-occur in the reporting. General articles about a celebrity or CEO will not falsely verify unrelated claims.

3. Catching Debunks & Exaggerated Numbers

Our system automatically identifies fact-check debunks published by professional newsrooms (Reuters Fact Check, AP) and verifies numbers, dates, and percentages against reported figures.
