Mathematical Architecture

Verification Methodology & Scoring Logic

Learn how VeriClaim calculates deterministic, reproducible corroboration scores without opaque black-box models.

1. Hybrid TF-IDF Vector Cosine Similarity

TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) measures how important specific keywords are to a document relative to a corpus. The mathematical cosine angle $\theta$ between the claim vector $\vec{A}$ and article vector $\vec{B}$ is computed as:

Similarity(A, B) = cos(θ) = (A · B) / (||A|| * ||B||)

Our hybrid algorithm combines Title-to-Title alignment (45%), Title-to-Body depth (30%), and Concept entity recall (25%) to achieve realistic calibration on short breaking news headlines.

2. Quantitative Fact-Discrepancy Formula

Many misinformation claims share 90% of words with real reports but distort key quantities (e.g. claiming $50 billion instead of $5 billion). The discrepancy ratio $\Delta$ is calculated as:

Δ = |Value_claim - Value_wire| / max(Value_claim, Value_wire)

If $\Delta > 0.15$ (a greater than 15% discrepancy in reported amounts), a fact conflict is flagged and a -35 point penalty is applied to prevent viral exaggerated claims from appearing credible.
