How VeriClaim Works

Simple explanation of our verification process

1. Live News Search

When you enter a claim, we simultaneously search 7 global news wire services (The Guardian, NewsAPI, Currents, Mediastack, GNews, NewsData, and WorldNews) to find recent stories about that topic.

2. Date & Author Verification

We identify when each story was published and who wrote it (e.g. journalist byline or news agency desk) so you can tell if a story is breaking news or an old rumor resurfacing.

3. Catching Exaggerated Numbers

Many fake news posts take a real story but exaggerate the numbers (e.g. claiming $50 billion instead of $5 billion). Our system checks specific numbers, dates, and percentages, applying an automatic penalty if numbers are wrong.
